⚡ Key Rules for Beginners
▸Rest 60–90 sec between sets. Don't rush.
▸Form over reps. 6 clean > 12 sloppy.
▸Drink water before, during, after.
▸Sleep 7–8h. That's when you get stronger.
▸Soreness is normal. Sharp pain — stop.
▸After 3–4 weeks, add reps gradually.
▸Protein: ~1.5g/kg = ~100g/day for you.
▸Consistency beats perfection. 80% is enough.
